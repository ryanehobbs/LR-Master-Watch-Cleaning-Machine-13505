## I.0 — What the machine does

The L&R Master is a **spinning-basket parts cleaner** for watch and clock movements. A motor drives a mesh **basket (BSK-002)** that holds the disassembled parts. The basket is lowered into a jar of cleaning or rinsing solution and spun (in both directions) so the fluid scrubs the parts; it is then moved to the next jar, and finally to a heated **drying chamber**. The motor head rides on a swiveling carrier so the operator can index the basket over each station in turn.

**Stations (4 wells, 2×2 grid):**
- **3 solution jar bays (BAS-003)** — glass jars of cleaning fluid, then rinse(s).
- **1 heater well (BAS-002)** — a tall tube that acts as a warm-air **drying chamber** (the heating element R1 sits below; the basket parks above it, never touching it).

## I.1 — Controls

| Control | Part | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed knob** (front, center of tongue) | Rheostat CTL-001 | Motor speed **and ON/OFF**. Full counter-clockwise = OFF. There is no separate power switch. |
| **Plunger button** (top of tongue) | Plunger switch CTL-003 | **Momentary REVERSE.** Released (out) = forward; press and hold = reverse. |
| **Toggle** (left side face) | Toggle switch CTL-004 | **Heater ON/OFF** (also lights the pilot). |
| **Red jewel** (upper-left of tongue) | Pilot lamp CTL-005/006 | Lights when the **heater is on**. |
| **Speed carrier** (rides the post) | Carrier NCK-002 | Swivels/locks to position the basket over each well; the clamp handle (NCK-003) raises/holds the motor. |

## I.2 — Setup

1. **Place the machine on a GFCI-protected outlet** on a stable, ventilated bench. (See Safety, I.4.)
2. **Fill the jars:** put your cleaning solution in the first jar bay and rinse solution(s) in the others, per your chosen watch-cleaning fluids. Seat each glass jar in a bay. The tapered rubber seal (PLT-002) on the platform deck wedges into the jar mouth to contain splashing.
   > ⚠️ **Use non-flammable / water-based solutions** — the motor sparks at the commutator (see I.4).
3. **Load the basket:** place the disassembled parts in the mesh basket (BSK-002); use the multi-movement holder inserts if cleaning several movements. Close the lid. Mount the basket on the drive spindle (SPN-002).
4. **Check the speed knob is at OFF** before plugging in.

---

## I.3 — Cleaning cycle (typical)

1. **Index over the first jar.** Swivel the carrier so the basket is centered over the cleaning jar, and lower it in.
2. **Start slow.** Plug in, then **sweep the speed knob up from OFF** to start the motor and set a moderate speed. (Starting from OFF is a soft-start — see I.4.)
3. **Agitate both directions.** Let it run forward, and **press-and-hold the plunger** periodically to reverse — alternating directions helps the fluid work through the parts. Run for your preferred cleaning time.
4. **Return to OFF, raise, and move to the next jar** (rinse). Repeat the spin/reverse agitation.
5. **Repeat through the remaining rinse jar(s).**
6. **Dry.** Index the basket over the **heater well**, switch the **toggle ON** (the red jewel lights), and spin the basket in the warm chamber to dry the parts. Give it enough time to dry fully, then switch the heater OFF.
7. **Shut down.** Return the speed knob to **OFF**, **then unplug.** Remove the basket and retrieve the parts.

## I.4 — Operating safety (read before use)

- **Non-flammable solutions only.** The motor is a brushed universal motor and **sparks at the commutator** — a normal ignition source. Never run it with volatile/flammable solvent vapor around it. Use water-based or non-flammable cleaning fluids and keep the area ventilated.
- **Keep it on a GFCI.** Liquids next to a mains motor is exactly the shock scenario a GFCI protects against. The machine is earth-grounded (restoration upgrade), which makes the GFCI fully effective.
- **Soft-start habit:** always have the **speed knob at OFF when you plug in and unplug**, and start the motor by sweeping the knob up. This avoids an inrush surge/spark and is gentler on the motor and brushes.
- **Don't force a seized basket or carrier** — free gently.
- **Hot chamber:** the heater well and dried parts are warm — handle accordingly.
- **The machine is live whenever plugged in** (the speed knob is the only on/off) — unplug it when not in use.
