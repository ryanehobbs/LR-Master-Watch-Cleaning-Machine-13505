[5580 Feather Grass Ln 2.m4a  (143s)  — Control Unit wiring layout (as-observed, verbal), 2026-06-25]

Voice memo June 25th 2026. I'm just going to kind of describe the electrical wiring layout on the L&R precision watch cleaning machine. So the first part, which is the wires that go up to the motor housing unit, there is a green, white and black wire. The green wire connects to — I don't know exactly what part of the rheostat, but it connects to the rheostat. The black and white wire, they connect to the plunger, which tells the motor to go forward or reverse. When you push the plunger it goes reverse; when it's out and it's default state, it's going in a forward direction. Then there is the mains, which goes into the actual wall socket. There's only two wires that come off that: there is a white wire and a black wire. The black wire goes into one of the heater / that heater element, and then the white wire goes to the rheostat. Now if we work our way around from the rheostat where the white wire plugs in, there's a black wire coming off the same lead, and that is going to the switch that controls the heater. So that black wire goes into the switch. And then there's another black wire that feeds off that same switch into the other side of this heater unit. And then from that heater unit there's another black wire that goes all the way down to the light bulb. And then from the light bulb, there is — from that same connection — there is a black wire that crosses over to the other side of the heater unit. And then there's a black wire that goes to what looks like the plunger. So there's a little bit of a cross-connecting there. Hopefully that all made sense.
